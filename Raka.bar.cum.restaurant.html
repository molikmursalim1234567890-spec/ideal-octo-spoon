Our Story

A Little More Than a Restaurant. A Place to Belong.

Tucked away on Nabasan Road in Bagnan, Rakha Bar Cum Restaurant is where the warmth of Bengali hospitality meets the polish of fine dining. From sizzling tandoori platters and rich Indian curries to wok-tossed Chinese favourites, every dish is crafted with fresh ingredients and served in a beautifully air-conditioned, family-friendly space.

Whether it’s a quiet anniversary dinner, a birthday celebration, or a relaxed evening at our premium bar — every visit is designed to become a memory.
